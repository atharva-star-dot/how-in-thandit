# Feature Specification: Winter Smog Family Assistant ("How In थंडीत")

**Feature Branch**: `001-winter-smog-advisor`

**Created**: 2026-11-22

**Status**: Draft

**Input**: User description: "Build 'How In थंडीत', a web app for families and communities dealing with severe winter smog. Users enter a city or allow location, and a household profile: members (children, elderly, people with respiratory conditions), commute mode (walk, two-wheeler, car, public transport), home type (apartment/independent house), whether they own a purifier, cooking fuel (LPG/induction/biomass), and budget level (low/medium/high). The app shows: 1. Today's air snapshot: AQI, PM2.5, category (Good to Hazardous), and a plain-language meaning. 2. A personalized daily plan generated by GenAI, split into: Morning/Commute routine, Indoor air quality hacks, Kids and elders care, Sustainable alternatives (reduce own emissions). 3. For every suggestion, an "Impact" explainer: what the action does, who benefits, and how big the effect is (Low/Medium/High), with the reason. 4. A 'Ask How In थंडीत' chat for follow-up questions. 5. A community mode: a shareable summary card for a housing society or school with today's AQI and top 3 actions. 6. Language toggle: English, हिंदी, मराठी. Out of scope for MVP: user accounts, database, push notifications, medical advice, hardware sensors."

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-11-22

- Q: Which external air quality data source strategy should the application use for retrieving live Indian city AQI and PM2.5 readings? → A: Option A (Open-Meteo Air Quality API with keyless PM2.5, PM10, AQI, and hourly forecasts, paired with Open-Meteo Geocoding API for city-to-coordinate lookup and seasonal baseline fallback).

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Local Air Quality Snapshot & Household Setup (Priority: P1)

As a parent or family caregiver in an urban area during winter, I want to quickly view my local air pollution levels and input my basic household profile so that I can understand how dangerous the air is right now without confusing scientific jargon or mandatory signups.

**Why this priority**: Without accurate local air quality data and household context, personalized recommendations cannot be generated. This forms the foundational MVP slice.

**Independent Test**: The user inputs a city or allows geolocation, selects basic household options, and immediately sees current AQI, PM2.5 reading, standard color-coded category (e.g., CPCB bands from Good to Severe/Hazardous), and a simple plain-language summary in the selected language.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a user opens the app for the first time, **When** they choose their city or allow device location, **Then** the app displays the latest verified AQI, PM2.5 value, severity badge, and an explanation of what the air quality means for daily life.
2. **Given** live air quality feeds are unreachable or sensor data is stale, **When** the snapshot loads, **Then** the app prominently displays a "Live Data Unavailable" notice and shows labeled general seasonal smog guidelines instead of estimating false numbers.
3. **Given** a user configures their household profile (vulnerable members, commute type, home type, purifier presence, cooking fuel, budget level), **When** they save changes, **Then** the profile is persisted exclusively within their browser storage without creating an account or sending profile identifiers to a server database.

---

### User Story 2 - Personalized Daily Smog Plan with Impact Explainers (Priority: P1)

As a household decision-maker, I want a tailored action plan for today divided into clear routine categories, where each suggestion explains why it works and how much impact it has, so that I can take practical, low-cost steps to protect my family.

**Why this priority**: Generic advice (e.g., "buy an expensive purifier" or "stay inside all day") fails Indian family realities. Actionable routines customized to family constraints deliver the core value proposition.

**Independent Test**: Given a completed profile and AQI status, the system generates a plan with four clear sections (Morning/Commute, Indoor Hacks, Kids & Elders Care, Sustainable Alternatives), where each item includes a dedicated "Impact" block (action, beneficiary, Low/Medium/High impact level, and approximate hedged reason).

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a family with children, an elderly member with respiratory sensitivity, a two-wheeler commute, and low budget, **When** the daily plan is generated under "Very Poor" AQI, **Then** the plan recommends specific commute precautions (e.g., well-fitted N95 masks, shifting departure times if feasible), affordable indoor barriers (damp cloth gap-sealing), vulnerable member protections, and combustion minimization without assuming expensive appliances.
2. **Given** any recommended action, **When** viewing that recommendation, **Then** it contains a distinct "Impact Explainer" detailing what the action does, who specifically benefits, an impact badge (Low / Medium / High), and a hedged explanation of why it helps without fabricating precise synthetic percentages.
3. **Given** any recommendation involving sensitive health conditions, **When** displayed, **Then** it includes an explicit non-medical advice disclaimer and advises consulting a physician for clinical symptoms.

---

### User Story 3 - "Ask How In थंडीत" Conversational Assistant (Priority: P2)

As a concerned elder or parent, I want to ask specific follow-up questions in my preferred language (e.g., "Should my child go to sports coaching today?" or "Does a wet towel across the window help?"), so that I can make informed situational decisions throughout the day.

**Why this priority**: Static checklists cannot address nuanced everyday situations that families encounter as pollution fluctuates.

**Independent Test**: The user types or selects a common question in the chat interface and receives a direct, contextual answer grounded in their profile and today's AQI, strictly observing medical non-diagnosis guardrails.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the user asks a situational question (e.g., "Can my grandfather go for a 6:00 AM walk today?"), **When** the chat assistant responds under "Severe" air conditions, **Then** it advises against early morning outdoor exertion, explains temperature inversion in simple terms, suggests afternoon indoor stretching, and reminds the user to speak to a doctor if respiratory distress occurs.
2. **Given** a user asks for medical diagnoses, medication changes, or dosage (e.g., "How much inhaler dose should I give?"), **When** processed, **Then** the assistant refuses to provide medical dosage or clinical diagnosis, shows a visible warning, and instructs immediate consultation with a certified doctor.
3. **Given** the chat is active, **When** the conversation continues, **Then** responses maintain conversational memory of the user's active household profile within the local session.

---

### User Story 4 - Community Summary Card & Multilingual Experience (Priority: P3)

As a resident association member, teacher, or community volunteer, I want to generate a clean, shareable summary card of today's air quality and top 3 collective actions in English, Hindi, or Marathi so that I can share it to our society WhatsApp group or school noticeboard.

**Why this priority**: Smog mitigation requires collective neighborhood awareness; shareable cards enable viral community adoption and mutual protection.

**Independent Test**: The user toggles between English, Hindi, and Marathi, and clicks "Generate Community Card", producing a formatted card showing city name, current AQI badge, date, top 3 community actions, and a one-click copy/share function.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a user selects Marathi (मराठी) or Hindi (हिंदी) on the language switcher, **When** the interface updates, **Then** all navigation labels, air snapshot summaries, personalized plan categories, and community card texts render naturally in that language.
2. **Given** current air conditions in a city, **When** the user opens "Community Mode", **Then** the app renders a compact, visual summary card featuring today's date, location, AQI level, and top 3 high-leverage community actions (e.g., stop waste burning, coordinate carpooling, moist-mop common stairwells).
3. **Given** the community card is displayed, **When** the user taps "Share / Copy", **Then** the card content is copied to clipboard as formatted text or prepared for instant messaging sharing with an attribution link.

---

### Edge Cases

- **Unavailable Sensor Station**: What happens when a user's specific district or tier-2/3 town has no active CPCB/sensor station? The system falls back to the nearest regional monitoring station or state capital index, clearly notifying the user of the geographic distance.
- **Corrupt or Unreachable API**: What happens when external weather/AQI services encounter network downtime? The app loads in graceful offline mode, informs the user with an intuitive message, and provides general seasonal winter guidelines labeled "Live Data Offline".
- **Local Storage Disabled / Private Browsing**: How does the app behave if browser cookies or `localStorage` are blocked? The app functions smoothly using an in-memory session state for that visit, notifying the user that settings will reset on refresh.
- **Extreme Weather Spikes (AQI > 500 / "Hazardous")**: What happens when pollution exceeds standard scale limits? The app triggers an urgent visual alert banner highlighting critical indoor stay-at-home precautions and vulnerable member protections.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: System MUST allow users to specify their location by searching for a city/district or by granting browser geolocation.
- **FR-002**: System MUST capture and locally persist the following household profile attributes:
  - Household members: Children (under 12), Elderly (60+), Individuals with asthma / respiratory conditions, Pregnant individuals.
  - Primary commute modes: Walking, Two-wheeler (bicycle/motorcycle), Open Auto-rickshaw, Car (AC/closed), Public bus/train.
  - Home structure: Multi-story Apartment vs. Independent ground-floor house.
  - Air filtration: Air purifier owned vs. No purifier.
  - Cooking fuel: LPG, Induction/Electric, or Biomass/Wood/Chulha.
  - Budget preference: Low (no-cost / DIY hacks), Medium, High.
- **FR-003**: System MUST fetch verified air quality data (current PM2.5, PM10, AQI index, and short hourly forecast) via the Open-Meteo Air Quality API (with city search resolved via Open-Meteo Geocoding API) and map it to standard Indian CPCB health bands:
  - Good (0–50), Satisfactory (51–100), Moderate (101–200), Poor (201–300), Very Poor (301–400), Severe / Hazardous (401–500+).
- **FR-004**: System MUST present a plain-language interpretation of the air snapshot tailored for non-technical users and elders.
- **FR-005**: If air quality data is stale, missing, or station connectivity fails, system MUST display an explicit "Live Data Unavailable" indicator and fall back to labeled general winter guidance.
- **FR-006**: System MUST generate an AI-tailored daily action plan containing four designated sections:
  1. *Morning & Commute Routine*
  2. *Indoor Air Quality Hacks*
  3. *Kids & Elders Care*
  4. *Sustainable Alternatives (Emissions Reduction)*
- **FR-007**: Every generated recommendation MUST include an "Impact Explainer" consisting of:
  - Action summary
  - Target beneficiaries (e.g., "Protects asthmatics & toddlers")
  - Impact level badge (`Low`, `Medium`, or `High`)
  - A calibrated "Why this helps" explanation with approximate, hedged effect descriptions without fabricated exact percentages.
- **FR-008**: System MUST provide an interactive conversational assistant ("Ask How In थंडीत") to answer contextual user queries regarding smog management.
- **FR-009**: The conversational assistant MUST enforce strict safety guardrails: refuse to formulate clinical diagnoses, prescribe medications, or suggest pharmaceutical dosing, and MUST append a persistent "not medical advice" notice while directing symptomatic users to a physician.
- **FR-010**: System MUST provide a "Community Mode" that generates a shareable digest card containing the date, location, AQI status badge, and top 3 community actions.
- **FR-011**: System MUST provide a one-touch language toggle supporting English, Hindi (हिंदी), and Marathi (मराठी), ensuring all static UI, plan generation prompts, and chat outputs conform to the chosen language.
- **FR-012**: System MUST operate with zero mandatory user registration or authentication and MUST NOT transmit or store personal household profiles or health tags on any central server database.

### Key Entities

- **Location**: Represents the selected geographic area (name, district, coordinates, active monitoring station identifier).
- **AirQualitySnapshot**: Represents current atmospheric conditions (AQI numeric value, PM2.5 metric, PM10 metric, CPCB category string, timestamp, data freshness status, plain-language description).
- **HouseholdProfile**: Represents client-stored user preferences (family member vulnerabilities list, commute method, dwelling type, purifier availability flag, cooking fuel type, budget sensitivity tier).
- **DailyPlan**: Represents the generated daily schedule (date, location reference, household profile reference, list of ActionItems grouped into the 4 functional categories).
- **ActionItem**: Represents an individual recommendation (title, category, action steps, impact rating [Low/Medium/High], target beneficiaries, hedged impact explanation).
- **CommunityCard**: Represents the shareable society/school digest (headline, date, AQI level, top 3 community interventions, share text payload).
- **ChatMessage**: Represents an interaction in the Q&A thread (sender role [user/assistant], message content, timestamp, safety disclaimers).

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: First-time users can complete location selection, configure their household profile, and view their personalized daily plan in under 90 seconds.
- **SC-002**: 100% of generated recommendations contain a valid Impact Explainer with a defined impact tier (Low/Medium/High) and a hedged qualitative rationale.
- **SC-003**: 100% of user queries asking for clinical diagnosis, medicine prescriptions, or dosing are safely intercepted with medical referral advisories and zero clinical claims.
- **SC-004**: Users can toggle between English, Hindi, and Marathi with instant UI adaptation and localized AI output.
- **SC-005**: 0% of user household profiles, names, or health tags are recorded in server logs or database stores, ensuring complete client-side data privacy.
- **SC-006**: Community cards can be generated and copied to clipboard in a single tap/click across mobile and desktop browsers.

---

## Assumptions

- **Target Audience**: Indian families, parents, working professionals, and housing society administrators residing in smog-affected areas with basic smartphone/web connectivity.
- **Sensor Feeds**: Public government or open monitoring station data (e.g., CPCB / OpenAQ / AQICN / Weather APIs) is accessible via server-side proxy requests with sufficient coverage for major Indian urban centers.
- **Browser Capabilities**: Target users run modern mobile or desktop browsers supporting standard CSS layout, Javascript, and `localStorage`.
- **MVP Boundaries**: Native mobile apps (iOS/Android store builds), hardware IoT sensors, hardware purifier telemetry, user accounts, relational databases, and SMS/push notification dispatchers are explicitly out of scope for the MVP.
